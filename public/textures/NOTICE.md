# Earth texture — provenance & license

**File:** `earth-natural.jpg`
**Base source:** Natural Earth I with Shaded Relief and Water (NE1_50M_SR_W), Natural Earth — authored by Tom Patterson & Nathaniel Vaughn Kelso (naturalearthdata.com / shadedrelief.com).
**Original:** 10800×5400 GeoTIFF, downscaled to 4096×2048.
**Post-processing (this repo):** ocean pixels flattened to a single flat teal (#3F7480) to remove bathymetry, and land warmed/darkened slightly (×[0.95, 0.86, 0.74]), to match the intended "dusty cartographic" globe aesthetic — tan shaded-relief land, flat teal ocean.
