## Theme

Lagoon was picked from the three mocks in `.hallmark/theme-preview.html`, with a muted dark
mode after the first dark version read too strong. Applied in `6be21be` by remapping the
ramps in `app/assets/css/tailwind.css`, so existing `sand`/`terra`/`ocean` classes pick it up.
